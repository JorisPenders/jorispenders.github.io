Today was a day of catching up with conference talks. One talk on my list for a while was the talk of Simon Wardley about Wardley Maps. It's a concept that helps to determine the strategy within your company, since it helps to reason about the products and services that you offer and think about the direction you should take.


## About Wardlay Maps
The main goal of the Wardley Maps is to turn diagrams into maps, by adding orientation. With this orentation, it helps to reason about the world. The Y-anchor of the maps represents the visibility of the component: from users, towards needs, listing the chain of components you need to achieve the thing for the usser. The other axis represents the movement or evolution: is something new, custom build, a product, or does it become more and more a commodity.

So, now you have a map. So what? Let's see what you can do with it if you realise that _everyhing evolves_. The map is not static, but things tend to move towards the right size: things will become more productionized. So, what is the space you want to invest in? Probably not the things that will become a commodity for your business, but things that make your business special.

To look into more detail about the things that will change, it is good to look at the climatic patterns that exist.

Some principles that you can apply on this spacE:
- Know your users
- Focus on user needs
- Know the details and the value/supply chion
- Understand what is being considered
- Use appropriate methods for custom build / product / commodity
- Everthing evolves
- Efficiency enables innovation
