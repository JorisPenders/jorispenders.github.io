Today is the day I really started to dig into research agents. With installing Antigravity I'm doing my first exercises with it. 

I'm a wheelchair user and getting a camping is always a challenge. I'm hoping that research agents can help me with finding suitable campings and planning my trips. 

And it appeared they can. Asking for PMR or adaptable campings and the agent is able to find suitable campings. I wrote a prompt that is able to create some markdown files with the results, and the first results are promising. 

I'm going to use this blog to document my findings and share my experiences with others. 

Stay tuned for more!
